"Mirror" in the owner's message is an instruction, not a feature name: restate their request back in clearer, cleaner words so they can confirm they were understood, then carry on.

**Why:** on 2026-10-15 the owner explained they often write unclear text and use "Mirror" to check they're understood. I misread it as the name of an idea ("mirror Swarm").

**How to apply:** open the reply with a short "What I understand you want:" restatement of their message; keep it plain, and don't add ideas of your own to it.
