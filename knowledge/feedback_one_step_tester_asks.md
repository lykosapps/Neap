Whatever we ask a tester for must be something they can provide whole in a
single step (one folder zipped, one recording, one pasted output), not a
sequence of changes and re-sends.

**Why:** testers on issue 1 have come back several times and could get
frustrated; the owner needs confidence each round is the last one it can be. The
Atlas Air plan listed Swarm's device plugin as the best source, but the
0.2.1.2 reply asked only for the settings file, so another round was needed.

**How to apply:** before drafting a tester reply, ask what single thing would
answer the open questions for good (the device's plugin folder beats a before
and after comparison), give exact steps for that one thing, and keep trying the
build as a separate optional ask. See [[feedback-headset-support-page]].
