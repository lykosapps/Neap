Since 2026-09-25 the project's bugs, features and to-dos live in `BACKLOG.md` at the repo root, linked from the README. It was seeded from every earlier Neap session, each item checked against main. It becomes GitHub issues when the repo is published.

**Why:** the owner asked for one place for "bugs, features, things to do" that he can see; memory notes are invisible to him.

**How to apply:** when the owner reports a bug or asks for something "for later", add a line to BACKLOG.md and commit it. When work lands, remove its line in the same commit. Do not keep duplicate to-do lists in memory; [[standard-checks-to-add]] and [[taskbar-quick-access-wanted]] are also in the backlog now.
