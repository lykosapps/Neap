When deciding what a headset has (which functions to show, hide or test), use Turtle Beach's own support page for that model as the reference — e.g. the Atlas Air's "Swarm II App (Desktop Version)" article on support.turtlebeach.com, which lists every Swarm II setting for it. Applies to every headset, not just the Atlas Air. Product pages are marketing and incomplete; a recording only shows what Neap could read.

**Why:** owner said so on 2026-10-08 after I hid Superhuman Hearing and treated noise gate as unlikely on the Atlas Air, both of which its support page lists.

**How to apply:** before changing a headset's function list in HeadsetModels, read its support article (the site renders with JavaScript, so use the built-in browser and wait a few seconds before reading the text), and record findings in FINDINGS.md marked *(research)*.
